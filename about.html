About Tejada Construction LLC

Tejada Construction LLC is a proud, family-owned and operated enterprise steadfastly serving the D.C., Maryland, and Northern Virginia (DMV) area. We were founded on the immutable principles of integrity, hard work, and a profound respect for the craft of building.

For years, our family has brought unparalleled dedication to the construction industry. We understand that we are not merely mixing concrete or laying stone; we are shaping the environments where families grow, businesses thrive, and communities connect. Our agency-style approach to client relations means you receive transparent communication, meticulous project planning, and a concierge-level experience from the initial blueprint to the final walk-through.
